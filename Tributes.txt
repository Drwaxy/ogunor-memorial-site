You became the grandfather I never had the opportunity to meet or know, and through you, I came to understand what it feels like to have that kind of love, guidance, and presence in my life. You filled a space in my heart that I never knew was empty, and for that, I will forever be grateful.
Your death came as a shock to me, and honestly, it is still difficult to accept that you are no longer here. There are some goodbyes the heart simply isn't prepared for. I wish we had more time. I wish I could hear your voice, see your smile, and experience your presence just one more time.
But as painful as this loss is, we cannot question God. We can only be grateful for the beautiful life you lived and the many lives you made better simply by being in them. You lived a good, meaningful, and fulfilling life, and you left behind memories that death can never erase
I am so proud to have called you my uncle. I am proud to have known you, to have loved you, and to have experienced the goodness of your heart. You were truly one of a kind my mannest man, Obuwetaku Orameyee.
Your wisdom will continue to speak through the people you taught. Your kindness will continue through the people you helped. Your legacy will live on through your family and everyone whose life you touched.
Rest peacefully, my beloved uncle. 🕊️
You may have left this world, but you will never leave our hearts.
Thank you for being the good man you were. Thank you for being my uncle. Thank you for being a grandfather in a way you may never have realized.
Until we meet again, Obuwetaku Orameyee, my mannest man. ❤️🕊️
May your beautiful soul rest in perfect peace.

Tribute to My Beloved Uncle, Obuwetaku

Chaii, Daddy… 💔

That cold and windy Friday morning in Calabar, I got the call that changed everything. I froze. I doubted it. I couldn't believe it. I kept hoping someone would tell me it wasn't true. I couldn't believe that the Easter celebration was the last time I would ever see you again. If I had known it would be our last time together, I would have held on a little longer, looked at you a little longer, and cherished every second even more.
Daddy, you were my best Uncle, and I wouldn't trade our relationship for anything in this world. You gave me the freedom to become whoever I wanted to be without making me feel judged. You taught me to be myself, even with all my lazy sides and stubborn moments. You accepted me exactly as I was, and that kind of love is something I will carry with me for the rest of my life.
From 2014 to 2026, we made so many beautiful memories together. Memories that I didn't know would one day become the things I would hold onto so tightly. I remember how you always begged my daddy to allow us to come back to the village. You were always happy to have us around. You loved me like your own daughter, and I felt that love in everything you did.
Ahhh, Daddy… I could still remember the sausage roll you always bought whenever you were returning from work. Those little things meant so much to me. I remember how you would save me whenever my mum wanted to beat me. 😂❤️ You were my safe place, my defender, my uncle, and in so many ways, a father figure to me.
Every time I came to the village, you made sure I could eat whatever I wanted. You made me feel at home. You made the village feel like a place of warmth, laughter, love, and beautiful memories.
Now, I find myself wishing I could go back to those days. I wish I could hear you call my name. I wish I could sit with you again, eat with you, laugh with you, and experience those simple moments that I never knew would become precious memories.
I miss you every single day, Daddy. And I know I will continue to miss you in ways words can never fully explain.
Your death has left a space in my heart that no one can fill. But even though you are gone, the love you gave me remains. The lessons you taught me remain. The memories we created remain. And your legacy will continue to live through everyone whose life you touched.
You lived, you loved, you gave, and you made a difference. That is the kind of life that deserves to be remembered forever.
Daddy, you will always be my favorite Uncle. ❤️
You will always be a beautiful part of my story.
You will always be the man who loved me like his own daughter.
Live long in our memories, Daddy. Your name, your kindness, your laughter, your wisdom, and your legacy will never be forgotten.

Until we meet again, Daddy…
I love you. I miss you. And I will never forget you. 💔🕊️

Ogunor Precious Oluebubechukwu
